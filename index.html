— PROFILE

Hello, I'm Lydia Ye

LY Portrait · images/protrait.png

I'm a Creative Interaction Designer with hands-on experience in user-centric design, 3D prototyping and cross-functional delivery. Currently in my final year of Interaction Design at the University of Sydney (Top 20 Global University).

As a recent graduate, I work across the full product cycle — from user research and journey mapping to high-fidelity Figma prototypes and tested design systems. My projects span VR mental health, mobile habit-building, and physical product design. I have delivered high-impact solutions, including a VR social anxiety platform that reduced user anxiety by 80% and an education smart wristband that improved teacher-student feedback efficiency by 30% across 20+ test participants and 2 local schools.
